Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information Astroseek (https://astroseek.solutions) collects, why we collect it, and how we handle it. By using the website you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Details you enter: name, date of birth, birth time and birth place, used to find your rashi and prepare reports.

  • Payment information: payments are handled by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, bank or UPI details. We receive only the payment status and payment ID.

How we use your information

  • To show your rashi and daily rashifal
  • To prepare and deliver yearly and lifetime reports and ebooks
  • To confirm payments and answer support requests
  • To keep the website safe and working properly

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal or birth details. We share information only with service providers needed to run the service (such as Razorpay for payments and Cloudflare for hosting), or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as needed to deliver your order, provide support, and meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your choices

Cookies

We use only the minimum cookies or similar technology needed for the site to work. We do not use them to track you across other websites.

Children

Changes to this policy
