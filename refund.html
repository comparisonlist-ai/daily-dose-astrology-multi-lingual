Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy applies to the digital ebooks sold on astroseek.solutions (for example, the Grahashanti ebook series).

1. Digital product

Our ebooks are digital files (PDF). After payment, the ebook is delivered to the email address you provide. No physical product is shipped.

2. Cancellation

Because the ebook is delivered digitally right after payment, orders cannot be cancelled once payment is completed.

3. Refunds

We do not offer refunds once the ebook has been delivered, because a digital file cannot be returned. However, we will resend the file or issue a refund in these cases:

  • You paid but did not receive the ebook.
  • You were charged more than once for the same ebook.
  • The file is damaged, will not open, or is not the ebook you purchased.

Please contact us within 7 days of the payment, with your name, payment details, and the email address used for the order.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. The time taken to show in your account depends on your bank or payment provider and is usually a few working days.

5. Guidance disclaimer

The content of our ebooks is offered as guidance based on astrological belief and tradition. It does not guarantee any result and is not a substitute for medical, legal, or financial advice. Dissatisfaction with a prediction or remedy is not a reason for a refund.
