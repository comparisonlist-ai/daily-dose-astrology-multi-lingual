Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Astroseek sells digital products only: ebooks, yearly reports and lifetime birth chart reports. Nothing is shipped.

No cancellation after delivery

Because digital products are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, orders cannot be cancelled and are not refundable once the download link or access has been delivered to you.

When we will refund or fix an order

  • Payment taken but product not delivered: we will deliver it, or refund you in full if we cannot.
  • Duplicate payment: we will refund the extra payment.
  • Damaged or unreadable file: we will send a working copy, or refund you if we cannot fix it.
  • Wrong birth details entered by you: write to us within 7 days of purchase and we will prepare the report again once with the corrected details.

How to ask

Write to dailyhoroscoperashifal@gmail.com within 7 days of the purchase. Please include your name, the email or phone used for the order, and the payment ID or a screenshot of the payment. We reply within 2 working days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show the money.

Free content

Daily rashifal and the rashi finder are free, so they are not eligible for refunds.
